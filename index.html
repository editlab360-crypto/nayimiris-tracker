💡 Scotch Bonnet / Nai Miris Cultivation Advisory

• NPK (14 Days): Top-dress with balanced fertilizer every two weeks to maintain continuous flower flushes and sturdy branching.
• Fungicide (7 Days): Anthracnose fruit rot spreads rapidly in tropical humidity. Spray on a strict 7-day cycle or re-apply after heavy rain.
• Hormones (10 Days): Spray blossom boosters / NAA to prevent premature flower drop during high temperatures.
